THE THIN ONE

You cannot outrun it. Hide before it sees you, or burn a flare. Headphones help.

What to do

The gate lock is electric and the generator outside is dry. Bring it four fuel cans: the old camp (east), the fallen pine (west), the well (north), the boulders (south-east). None of them is just lying there. Then start it and leave by the gate, south. The map is on the board by the door.

My camcorder is on the table. Take it. It sees in the dark for as long as the battery lasts.

Staying alive

Watching it doesn't stop it any more, and neither does the lantern. You can't outrun it. Two things work: get into a hiding place before it sees you go in (the hollow logs with the pale fungus, under the bunk here), or throw a flare. I left you one. It comes in here now.
