Gambit Arena

Chess where every capture is a first-person duel. Play the computer, or a friend online with a match code. Move by the normal rules, then fight for the square.

  • Captures are duelsThe winner holds the square and the loser leaves the board, so a Pawn can beat a Queen.
  • No check, no checkmateYou win only by defeating the enemy King in a duel.
  • Normal chess movesCastling, en passant and promotion all work. Pawns promote to a Queen.
  • The board is the arenaEvery other piece stays on its square as cover during a duel.
  • Aim for the headThe top of every piece takes bonus damage. A scoped Rook headshot is an instant kill.
  • Cover healsGo four seconds without being hit and your health climbs back.
